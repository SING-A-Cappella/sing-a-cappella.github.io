Ways to support the festival

Sponsor the festival

Your name on the festival, on stage and online, for the weekend and the year of listings that follows. We tailor this to what matters to you.

Advertise in the program

Our program is in the hands of everyone at the festival and stays online for a full year afterwards.

Donate a prize or an auction item

Silent auction items and prizes raise money on the night and put your business in front of a friendly crowd.

Support Education Week

Send our clinicians into high schools. This is the part of our work that reaches young singers who might never buy a ticket.
